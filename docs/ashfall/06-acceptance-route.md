# Ashfall acceptance route

Updated 2026-10-24. Current contract: [Ashfall lock](08-lock-contract.md).
This replaces the original Solar-Array-only proposal. No resource cheat is required.

## Ordinary player route

1. In a new browser profile, choose **BEGIN GALAXY**. Select Ashfall in the Atlas,
   **COMMIT TRAVEL**, then **ACKNOWLEDGE ARRIVAL**.
2. **DESCEND TO COLONY**. Walk forward from the pad and left toward the Quartermaster.
   Use **Z / Interact** to talk and open the stock. At zero metal, confirming the
   Travel Supply row must explain the 20-metal shortfall without changing the save.
3. Return to the pad and open **COLONY MANAGEMENT**, or open **MANAGE COLONY** from
   the Atlas. Work salvage. Each shift adds 40 colony metal and one ship travel
   supply and advances one normal world cycle.
4. Commission **Solar Array → Mine → Habitat Module → Farm → Water Purifier** as
   each becomes affordable. Continue work until all five operate and ten settlers
   have arrived. The measured zero-stock route takes 25 shifts. Keep the existing
   prices and growth rules; waiting on a screen does not advance world time.
5. Descend again to see the finished building silhouettes. Meet the Overseer in
   the central plaza. The conversation must report the actual population, five
   operational buildings, power and remaining metal.
6. Visit the Quartermaster. Confirm **Travel Supply**: exactly 20 colony metal is
   removed and one ship supply is added. Ship credits and Legacy inventory stay
   unchanged. Holding Z must not repeat the exchange; release and press again for
   a second trade. Check one short touch tap as well.
7. Select **LEAVE**, walk back to the pad and **Take Off**. Travel to Vanguard and
   return to Ashfall. Reload, continue and descend. The five buildings, population,
   remaining metal and travel supply must reflect the journey and persisted trades.

## Compatibility limits

Ashfall Primary is the only descendable Galaxy colony. Clock projection stays at
07:00, and building geometry refreshes on descent. Saves remain browser-local.
The exchange uses the existing synchronous save-store boundary; it does not claim
cross-tab transactional locking. Galaxy consumable shops and social negotiation
are deferred, while Legacy shops keep their existing catalog and prices.
